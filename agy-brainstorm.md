## 5 Jev-Centered World-Changing SaaS Products

**1. Rebound**
`rebound.io` — *Catch the efficiency trap before it eats your sustainability gains.*
- Will this efficiency improvement increase net resource consumption via behavioral rebound?
- Which user segments are most likely to expand usage once friction drops?
- At what efficiency threshold does the Jevons effect flip savings into overshoot?

---

**2. CeilingCheck**
`ceilingcheck.com` — *Set hard output caps before you scale, not after.*
- What absolute cap should we impose so efficiency gains don't become a growth excuse?
- Which organizational incentives currently reward volume over sufficiency?
- If efficiency doubles, what governance prevents the freed capacity from being redeployed?

---

**3. Sufficiency**
`sufficiency.ai` — *Replace "use less per unit" with "use less, full stop."*
- What is the minimum viable resource envelope for this outcome, ignoring cost pressure?
- Does optimizing this process signal virtue while masking absolute growth?
- Which metric, if we stopped optimizing it, would most reduce total footprint?

---

**4. DemandCurve**
`demandcurve.co` — *Model the consumption surge before your product ships.*
- How elastic is demand for this service once the price-per-unit falls?
- What second-order markets get unlocked — and what do they consume?
- Which policy lever (price floor, quota, tax) closes the rebound loop for your category?

---

**5. Paradox**
`paradox.run` — *Board-level dashboards that show what efficiency is really costing you.*
- Is total consumption falling or just intensity-per-unit — and do stakeholders know the difference?
- Which efficiency KPIs are being gamed to look green while absolute outputs rise?
- Where should we deliberately *not* improve efficiency to avoid triggering demand expansion?
